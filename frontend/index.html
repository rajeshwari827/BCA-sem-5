About HungerFree

Together we can reduce food waste and feed people in need.

Share Food, Spread Happiness

Welcome to a community dedicated to ending local hunger. This platform makes it incredibly simple to share your surplus food with neighbors in need. Whether you are a business or an individual, your donations make an immediate difference.

Reduce Food Waste

Every day, millions of children go to bed hungry while good food goes to waste. We connect homes, restaurants and grocery stores with nearby NGOs.

Building Better Communities

We empower citizens, volunteers and businesses to deliver meals to people in need and make food distribution simple.

Every Meal Matters

Every donation helps reduce hunger and creates a better tomorrow. Together we can make sure no food goes to waste.

🌱 HungerFree Initiative

Don't Waste Food.
Feed Lives.

HungerFree connects restaurants, hotels, caterers, households and food donors with NGOs, old age homes and orphanages to ensure surplus food reaches people in need instead of going to waste.
